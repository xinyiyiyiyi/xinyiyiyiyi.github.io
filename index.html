👋 Hi! I’m Xinyi Yi, a PhD student in Engineering at the University of Cambridge, Churchill College.
My research focuses on the optimal control and coordination of multi-energy systems, with a current emphasis on district heating networks, energy sharing, and data-driven temperature regulation.

I am currently supervised by Prof. Ioannis Lestas.
During my master’s at Tsinghua University, I worked under the guidance of Prof. Ye Guo, and during my undergraduate studies at North China Electric Power University, I was jointly advised by Prof. Cheng Yu and Prof. Ming Zhou.
