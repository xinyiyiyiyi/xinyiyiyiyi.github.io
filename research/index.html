🎯 A Closed-loop Vision: From Markets to Control and Back#

My research explores how distributed control mechanisms can support real-world operation and pricing in multi-energy systems. I aim to bridge physical dynamics with market coordination, especially across electricity and heat sectors.

🔍 From Market Design to System Control#

My academic journey began with the study of multi-energy system scheduling and market-based pricing mechanisms, particularly in electricity and district heating systems. During my undergraduate and master’s training, I focused on how coordinated optimization of electricity and heat flows could support more efficient and equitable energy market pricing, including energy and reserve markets.

In my PhD, I shifted focus toward system control theory, especially on the shorter time scale of ancillary services such as frequency regulation. My current research emphasizes the co-regulation of frequency and temperature using methods such as energy-sharing controllers, LQR formulations, and Port-Hamiltonian modeling.

While I am currently focused on building robust technical foundations, I ultimately aim to return to the market layer, enabling physically sound yet economically aligned control strategies. My long-term goal is to enable control algorithms that can directly interact with market mechanisms, completing a loop from market design → distributed control → market integration.

From economic coordination to distributed control — and back again.

🔌 Coupled Electric–Thermal Control#

I design controllers that co-regulate power system frequency and thermal system temperature in combined heat and power (CHP) networks, including:

  • Secondary frequency control via PH systems
  • Energy-sharing temperature regulation
  • Heat pump integration and transient-aware feedback

📊 Energy Sharing under Uncertainty#

My recent work investigates steady-state and transient temperature control without demand forecasts:

  • Convex steady-state problem formulation
  • Demand-independent energy-sharing regulators
  • Theoretical guarantees on convergence and performance

🧠 Data-driven Temperature Regulation#

To enable adaptivity and deployment, I explore learning-based control strategies:

  • LQR with unknown dynamics
  • Policy iteration with stability
  • Learning-based control of pipeline networks

🔁 Long-term Vision#

My future work aims to close the loop between system control and market mechanisms, by:

  • Embedding control objectives into market clearing
  • Designing control-compatible pricing models
  • Enabling distributed participation in ancillary markets

Bridging real-world control and energy markets.
